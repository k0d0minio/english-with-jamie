French uses articles more than English, which is why *the nature* or *the life* sound off. Think: specific or general? Countable or not? First mention or known?

## a / an: one of many, first mention, jobs
*I met **a** girl from Toronto. She's **a** nurse. It's **an** hour away.* (*an* before a vowel **sound**: an hour, a university.)
