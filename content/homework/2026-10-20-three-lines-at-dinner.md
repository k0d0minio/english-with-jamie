At one dinner this week, say three of the table lines out loud, in English, to whoever is there (they can answer in French, that's fine): one about the food, one *could I*, one *I'm good, thanks*. Then record a thirty-second voice note introducing yourself the way you would to the host family, using *for* with a length of time at least once. Bring the voice note to Tuesday's lesson. It's the first thing you'll do over there; by the time it's real it should be boring.
