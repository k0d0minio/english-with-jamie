Conditionals are *if* sentences. The tense in the if-clause moves one step back as things become less real.

| Type | if-clause | main clause | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | present | present | always true |
| **First** | present | will + verb | real possibility |
| **Second** | past | would + verb | imaginary now |
| **Third** | past perfect | would have + participle | imaginary past |

- *If you heat ice, it melts.*
- *If it snows tomorrow, school will be cancelled.*
- *If I had a car, I'd drive to school.* (I don't)
- *If I'd known, I would have come.* (I didn't)

## Mixed

- *If I **had taken** the earlier train, I **wouldn't be** stuck now.* (past → present)
- *If I **weren't** so shy, I **would have spoken** to her.* (present → past)

## What people actually say

- *If I **were** you, I'd apologise.* (*was* is fine in speech)
- *Unless you hurry, you'll miss it.*
- *As long as you're back by ten, fine.*
- *What if we left early? Suppose it rains?*
- *Should you need anything, just ask.* (formal)

## Errors

- ~~If I will see him~~ → *If I see him, I'll tell him.*
- ~~If I would have known~~ → *If I had known.*
- ~~I wish I would be taller~~ → *I wish I were taller.*

## Practice

1. If I have a free weekend, …
2. If I could live anywhere, …
3. If I hadn't started these lessons, …
