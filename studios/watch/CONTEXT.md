# studios/watch — things to watch

Entered when Jamie asks for viewing suggestions, or after the student has said what she
likes. Suggestions are proposals; Jamie curates. Work from the repo root.

## Inputs

| Layer | File | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | [`../_shared/learner.md`](../_shared/learner.md) · [`../_shared/voice.md`](../_shared/voice.md) | Taste, level, tone |
| 3 | [`../_shared/formats.md`](../_shared/formats.md) § Watch | File shape |
| 4 | `intake/*.md` | What she said she likes; what Jamie wants covered (accents, Canada, school life) |
| 4 | `content/watch/` — frontmatter only | Don't repeat |

## Process

1. **Three to five entries per run**, never a dump. Mix one *comfortable* with the rest
   *stretch*. Prefer things with natural teenage speech, Canadian or varied accents, and
   subjects she cares about (animals, making things, the outdoors, music).
2. **Say why in her terms**, two or three sentences: what she will hear, what to listen
   for, one phrase to steal. Mention the platform only if you are sure.
3. **No spoilers, no age-inappropriate picks.** She is a minor.
4. **Save** as `content/watch/<slug>.md` with `draft: true`.

## Output

`content/watch/<slug>.md`, one per title.

## Gate (Jamie's)

- [ ] Jamie checks availability and suitability, removes `draft: true` or deletes the file.

## Done when

Entries saved and listed back with their level.
