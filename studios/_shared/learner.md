# The learner

Deliberately non-identifying: this repo is public.

- Teenager in secondary school, strong academic record, tested at **CEFR B2**.
- Native French speaker (Belgium). Expect French-shaped errors: present perfect vs past
  simple, article overuse (*the nature*), *since/for*, false friends (*actually*,
  *eventually*, *sensible*), silent-letter and *th* pronunciation, word stress.
- Preparing for a **six-month school exchange in Canada (Ottawa)**: living with a host
  family, attending a local secondary school, from late August.
- Confident on paper, less so out loud. Thoughtful, kind, a maker (knits, makes things by
  hand), loves animals, comfortable in her own company. Dislikes being talked down to.
- Goal of the lessons: feel comfortable speaking from day one over there. Conversation
  over exam technique.

## What that means for content

- Assume B2 reading; target B2+ listening and speaking. Don't simplify vocabulary; do
  explain idiom and register.
- Every lesson earns its place by answering "what will she actually have to say or
  understand in Ottawa?"
- Canadian English where it differs: *washroom*, *grade 10*, *loonie*, *eh*, *sorry*
  culture, Celsius with Fahrenheit awareness, *pop* vs *soda*.
- Topics she will enjoy: animals and nature, making things, music, series, the outdoors.
