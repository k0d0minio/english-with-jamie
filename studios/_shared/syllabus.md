# Syllabus — the arc to departure

One theme per lesson, roughly one lesson a week, in the order she will meet the
situations. Reorder freely; this is a map, not a timetable. Tick nothing here; the
lesson files' `status` says what has been taught.

| # | Theme | What she must be able to do | Grammar to lean on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First evening with the host family | Introduce herself, ask about the house and its rules, say what she likes and doesn't eat | present simple, polite requests (*could I…*) |
| 2 | Small talk and keeping it going | Answer *how was your day?*, ask back, react (*no way*, *that's fair*) | question tags, present perfect for news |
| 3 | School, day one | Understand a timetable, ask where things are, explain she is an exchange student | indirect questions, *going to* |
| 4 | In class | Ask a teacher to repeat or explain, say she doesn't understand, hand in work | modals of permission, reported instructions |
| 5 | Making friends | Join a conversation, suggest doing something, say no kindly | *shall we / fancy / how about*, softeners |
| 6 | Shops, money, food | Order, ask for the washroom, understand tax and tipping, read a menu | countables, *how much / many*, numbers out loud |
| 7 | Getting around | Buses, the O-Train, asking for directions, being lost | prepositions of place, imperatives |
| 8 | Phone and admin | Make an appointment, give her details, spell names, understand a recorded menu | spelling aloud, dates and times, future arrangements |
| 9 | Feeling off | Describe symptoms, homesickness, ask for help, say she needs a minute | *feel / be / have* + condition, present perfect continuous |
| 10 | Telling stories from home | Describe Belgium, her family, a funny thing that happened | narrative tenses, sequencing |
| 11 | Canadian English and the sorry culture | Recognise *eh*, *loonie*, *double-double*; apologise and accept apologies the Canadian way | register, idiom |
| 12 | Opinions and disagreeing nicely | Give a view in class, disagree without conflict, hedge | *I'd say*, *I'm not sure that…*, second conditional |
| 13 | Winter | Weather small talk, clothes, what to do when it is minus twenty | comparatives, advice modals |
| 14 | The last stretch | Thank the host family, keep in touch, talk about what changed | present perfect, future forms |

Threads that run through every lesson: listening at natural speed, pronunciation of the
five sounds French speakers miss, and one new idiom she will actually hear.
